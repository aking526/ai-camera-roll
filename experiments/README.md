# Evaluation iterations

These folders preserve dated experiment results. New experiments belong in new
folders; they should not overwrite earlier images or scores.

| Record | Question | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Iteration 01: portrait retention](iteration_01_portrait_retention_2026-11-04/README.md) | Does resupplying Maya's portrait during the second pass reduce facial drift? | Partial numerical recovery in three scenes, wrong-person editing in October, and no average object-similarity gain. |

The historical pilot remains in `output/` and `evaluation/results/`. Its images
and scores are also included in the self-contained iteration package for sharing.
“Iteration 01” identifies this controlled experiment, not completion of the
course's required number of iterations.
